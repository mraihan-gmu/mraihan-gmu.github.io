Beyond the CV

Personal

Some context about me that does not fit neatly into a CV.

A few things about me that the CV does not cover. I plan to keep adding to this page over time.

Where I come from
  • 🌏 I grew up in Bangladesh and studied at the Islamic University of Technology in Gazipur before moving to the United States for graduate school.
  • 🧭 A lot of my research grows out of that background: I keep asking why language models work so well in English and Python and so poorly in Bangla or in less common programming languages.
Outside research
  • 🤝 I served as Vice-President of the Bangladesh Graduate Student Association at George Mason, after a year as its Director of Sports.
  • 🏑 Organizing student sports and community events is something I genuinely enjoy, not just a line on a CV.
  • 🧑‍🏫 I like mentoring early-stage students; some of my favorite results came from work I did alongside first-year Ph.D. students.
  • 🛠️ I am an open-source person at heart: most of my benchmarks, corpora, and model weights are public so others can build on them.
